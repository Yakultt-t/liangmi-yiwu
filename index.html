两咪一屋把家里猫咪用的喂食器、猫砂盆、饮水机放进一个 App：看状态、看画面、改喂食计划、收提醒。数据只在你自己家的网络里流转。

它不能单独使用，需要配合开源项目 petkit-local：先在家里的 NAS、树莓派或任何一台能跑 Docker 的机器上搭好它，并把小佩（PetKit）设备接上去。

个人开发，与小佩官方没有关系。支持 iPhone、iPad、Mac 和 Apple Watch。

Liang Mi Yi Wu ("Two Cats, One Home") puts your cat's feeder, litter box and water fountain in one app: status, live view, feeding schedule and alerts. Your data stays on your own home network.

The app cannot be used on its own. It works together with the open-source project petkit-local, which you run on a NAS, Raspberry Pi or any machine with Docker, with your PetKit devices connected to it.

An independent personal project, not affiliated with PetKit. For iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.
