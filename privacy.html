首页隐私政策技术支持English

两咪一屋的设备数据只在你自己家里的 petkit-local 服务器上。账号是可选的：不登录也能在家里照常使用。

  • 不登录时，开发者收不到、也不保存你的任何数据。
  • 登录后，我们只保存下面“账号”一节列出的那几样，用来认出是你、记住你绑了哪个家、分享给了谁。
  • 没有广告、没有统计分析、不跟踪你。

数据在哪里

  • 设备状态、喂食计划、如厕和体重记录、摄像头画面和录像：保存在你自己家里的服务器上。App 只是从它那里读取和显示，在家走局域网，在外走你自己配置的外网入口。
  • App 的设置（名称、配色、服务器地址、设备显示名称）：只保存在你的设备上；装了手表 App 的话会同步到你的 Apple Watch。
  • 麦克风：只在你按住“说话”时使用，声音直接发到你家里的设备，不录音、不上传。

账号（可选）

你可以通过 Apple 登录或邮箱验证码登录，用来把自己家的服务器绑到账号上、分享给家人朋友。登录后账号服务会保存：

  • 邮箱：邮箱登录时你填的邮箱；通过 Apple 登录时苹果提供的邮箱（你可以选择隐藏邮箱），以及苹果给本 App 的匿名用户标识。
  • 名字：你自己填的、显示在成员名单里的名字（可以不填）。
  • 你绑定的家：家的名字、你家服务器的地址、设备的名字和型号、成员名单和各自的权限。
  • 登录状态：用来保持登录的令牌（只保存它的校验值）。

账号服务不保存设备状态、如厕和喂食记录、摄像头画面和录像，这些不经过账号服务。账号服务只给家里的成员签发一小时有效的“通行证”，由你自己家的服务器检查后放行。

这些数据存放在 Cloudflare 提供的服务器上。发送邮箱验证码时，你的邮箱地址会交给发信服务商用于投递这一封邮件。除此之外不提供给任何第三方，也不用于广告或分析。

删除：在 App 的“设置 → 账号 → 注销账号”里可以随时删除账号，账号、绑定的家和分享关系会立即全部删除，不能恢复。

推送通知

如果你允许通知，App 会把这台设备的推送令牌交给你自己家里的服务器。有事发生时（比如猫上厕所了、出粮失败），由你的服务器经 Apple 推送通知服务（APNs）把通知发到你的设备。通知内容会经过 Apple 的服务器，除此之外不经过任何第三方。

第三方

App 不包含任何第三方 SDK。

联系

有疑问请到技术支持页面联系。

Privacy Policy

Your device data stays on the petkit-local server that you run in your own home. An account is optional: the app works at home without signing in.

  • If you do not sign in, the developer does not receive or store any of your data.
  • If you sign in, we store only the items listed under “Account” below, to recognise you, remember which home you linked and who you shared it with.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Where your data lives

  • Device status, feeding schedules, litter box and weight records, camera video are stored on your own home server. The app only reads and displays them, over your local network at home or through the remote entry point you configure yourself.
  • App settings (name, colours, server address, device display names) are stored only on your device, and synced to your Apple Watch if the watch app is installed.
  • Microphone is used only while you hold the talk button. Audio is sent directly to your own device at home; it is not recorded or uploaded.

Account (optional)

You can sign in with Apple or with an email verification code in order to link your own home server to an account and share it with family or friends. When you sign in, the account service stores:

  • Email address: the address you enter for email sign-in; for Sign in with Apple, the address Apple provides (you may choose to hide your email) and the anonymous user identifier Apple issues for this app.
  • Name: an optional display name you enter, shown in the member list.
  • Homes you link: the home's name, your home server's addresses, device names and models, the member list and each member's permission.
  • Sign-in state: a session token (only its hash is stored).

The account service does not store device status, litter box or feeding records, or camera video; none of that passes through it. It only issues one-hour passes to members of a home, which your own home server verifies before allowing access.

This data is stored on servers provided by Cloudflare. When a verification code is sent, your email address is passed to an email delivery provider solely to deliver that message. It is not shared with any other third party and is not used for advertising or analytics.

Deletion: you can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Account → Delete account. The account, the homes you linked and all sharing are deleted immediately and cannot be restored.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, the app gives this device's push token to your own home server. When something happens, your server sends the notification through Apple Push Notification service (APNs). Notification content passes through Apple's servers and no other third party.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs.
